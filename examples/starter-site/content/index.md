# Weather Station

This small site documents an illustrative weather station API. Start with the [reading guide](guide/reading-a-reading/) to follow one measurement, or open the [API reference](reference/) for its contract.

![A simple station mast with a thermometer and wind vane.](assets/station.svg)

## What's here

- A short authored guide with ordinary Markdown links.
- One OpenAPI operation and one named model.
- A built-in Botanist Dark and Light theme pair.
- A small author-owned station illustration and downloadable sample readings.
